Research

CASLAB tackles challenging problems across the spectrum of modern computer architecture and systems. While our research addresses diverse emerging workloads—spanning Generative AI & LLMs, Cloud & Serverless Infrastructure, Billion-Scale Vector Search, and Hardware-level Security—all of our work is deeply unified through the foundational principles of Computer Architecture, Next-Generation Memory Hierarchies, Operating Systems, and Hardware/Software Co-Design.
